Hometown Serenity / Privacy

Your privacy,
plainly.

What this website collects, what happens when you get in touch, and how your sessions stay private.

This website

This site does not use contact forms or advertising trackers, and it does not set browser cookies. It is hosted by Netlify, which processes technical request data, such as IP addresses and the pages requested, to deliver, protect, and monitor the website. Hosting reports may include aggregate traffic and usage statistics.

Fonts and images are served from this website itself, not from third-party services.

Booking and getting in touch

Discovery calls are booked through Google Calendar's appointment page, which is run by Google under its own privacy policy. When you book, call, text, or email, Ashley receives the details you choose to share, such as your name, contact information, and a note about what brings you here.

Ashley uses that information only to respond to you, schedule and hold appointments, and keep basic records of your sessions. It is never sold or shared for marketing.

Your sessions

What you share in a session stays between you and Ashley. She will not share it without your permission, except where the law requires it or where someone's safety is at serious risk.

Online sessions take place over Zoom, which is run by Zoom under its own privacy policy. Choose a private space where you won't be overheard.

Details about session records, recording consent, and retention periods are provided in your client disclosure and consent documents. Please contact Ashley with questions about how your information is handled.

Your choices

You can ask Ashley what information she holds about you, ask her to correct it, or ask her to delete it when it no longer needs to be kept. Email ashleyromero@hometownserenity.com or call (870) 750-1275.
