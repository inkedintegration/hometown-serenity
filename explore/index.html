08 topics

Medical & clinical questions

Topics to discuss with the professional responsible for your care.

  • Chronic pain

    Hypnosis has been studied for pain; discuss options with your treating clinician.

  • Irritable bowel syndrome

    Gut-directed hypnotherapy is a specific clinical approach, not a general wellness session.

  • Smoking cessation

    Research findings are mixed; discuss a complete quit plan with a healthcare professional.

  • Hot flashes

    Hypnosis has been studied, with limited favorable evidence for some menopausal symptoms.

  • Anxiety and panic attacks

    A clinical assessment can help identify appropriate care for persistent symptoms.

  • Trauma

    Ask a qualified mental health professional about suitable support and practitioner training.

  • Substance use and addiction

    Treatment planning belongs with qualified addiction and healthcare professionals.

  • Surgery and childbirth

    Any use of hypnosis around a procedure belongs within the medical care plan.
