Hypnosis for Focus & Performance

Room to
concentrate.

An exam, a performance, a game, or a project you keep circling: focus often feels hardest when something matters most.

Hypnotherapy for focus & performance

Be specific. It might be the first minute on stage, the night before an exam, the free throw, or the blank page. Describe what you notice in your thoughts and in your body as that moment gets closer.

Then picture what going well would look like. A realistic goal, such as starting a study session without delay or walking on stage feeling steadier, gives a conversation a clear place to begin.

Rehearsal, imagery, and attention

Students, performers, and athletes sometimes use mental rehearsal, imagery, and relaxation as part of their preparation. Approaches vary widely, and no method guarantees how a particular day will go.

Ask Ashley how she works with focus and performance goals, and how session work could fit alongside your coaching, teaching, or training.

Mind and body under pressure

Pressure tends to show up in the body: a racing heart, shallow breathing, tight shoulders. A somatic perspective pays attention to those signals with curiosity, rather than treating them as something to fight.

Questions to bring

  • How do you approach focus, nerves, and performance?
  • How far ahead of an event should we start?
  • Is there anything I can practice between sessions?
  • How would this fit with my coach, teacher, or training plan?

Common questions

Is this the same as treating ADHD or a learning difference?+

No. Attention difficulties that affect school, work, or relationships deserve assessment by a qualified healthcare professional. Hypnotherapy is not a substitute for that evaluation or for treatment.

How soon before an event should I reach out?+

Talk with Ashley about your timeline. Starting well ahead of an important date leaves more room to practice and adjust.
