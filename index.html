Meet the person behind the practice

A warm hello.
I'm Ashley.

Ashley Romero, CHt, CAHA.
Founder of Hometown Serenity in Melbourne, Arkansas.

I was about to start my final semester for a BA in Culinary Arts. That was the plan. Solid, practical, figured out. And then life had other ideas.

Here's what I learned the hard way: my own healing journey taught me things no classroom ever could. And it showed me that I'd been chasing the wrong thing. Since I was a kid, I wanted to understand how the mind and body actually work together. How to help people find their way back to themselves. I'd just… forgotten about it somewhere along the way.

When HMI's mind and body psychology program came across my radar, it felt like a door opening to something I'd left behind. Not in spite of what I'd lived through, but because of it. My healing gave me my life back. And it showed me exactly what I needed to do with it.

So that's where I am now. Building care informed by the nervous system from the ground up, with clinical training, lived experience, and a stubborn belief that real healing lives in the body first. That's what NeuroRegulated Integration™︎ is. It's not theory I'm teaching you. It's the framework that carried me out and back to myself. Now it's here for you.

Are sessions available online?+

Can I meet Ashley in person?+

Yes. In-person hypnotherapy sessions are available in Melbourne, Arkansas. Contact Ashley to arrange an appointment and confirm the meeting location.

What happens in a session?+

Start with a conversation about your questions and what you would like to explore. Ask Ashley how her somatic hypnotherapy approach relates to your goals, and what your session will involve. Read about preparing for a session.
