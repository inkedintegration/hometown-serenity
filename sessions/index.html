A little reassurance

Common first-session
questions.

Will I lose control or become unconscious?+

Hypnosis is not the same as being unconscious. People describe the experience differently; you can communicate with Ashley and pause or stop at any time.

Is hypnosis sleep or magic?+

No. It is a guided exercise in focused attention and relaxation. The words used during an exercise do not mean you are biologically asleep, and nothing supernatural is happening.

What if my mind wanders?+

That is common. You can notice thoughts, sounds, or sensations and return your attention when you are ready. There is no single required feeling or response.

Do I have to talk about anything private?+

No. You choose what you want to share. A session is collaborative, and you can ask questions or decline an exercise.
